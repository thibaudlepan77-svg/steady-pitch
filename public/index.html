Why cents matter

In music, a cent is 1/100th of a semitone. It's the smallest interval most people can hear. When you're 25 cents off, you're a quarter tone away from the right pitch - enough to sound noticeably out of tune.

Steady Pitch shows your error in cents so you can see exactly how close you are. The goal isn't perfect 0 cents every time - even professional singers vary by 10-15 cents. The goal is consistency and control.

2. Practice for short sessions - 10-15 minutes is better than an hour

3. Focus on one skill at a time - matching pitch, then holding notes, then intervals

4. Record yourself occasionally to hear your progress

5. Be patient - ear training is a skill that develops over time
