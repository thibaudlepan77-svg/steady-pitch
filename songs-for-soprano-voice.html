Songs for a soprano voice

271 songs whose melody stays inside C4 to C6 from start to finish, out of 432 measured. Allow three semitones of transposition either way and it becomes 351.

Slightly fewer songs than the mezzo list, and the reason is the bottom rather than the top. Plenty of popular melody dips below C4 for a line or two, which puts it outside this band even when the high notes are easy. Transposing up a tone fixes most of those.

Why a voice type is a bad way to pick songs

A voice type is not a range. It is where your voice sits happily and where it changes gear, and two people called soprano can have their break in different places. A choir director put it to me more bluntly than that, and he was right. Using C4 to C6 as a filter is a starting point, not a verdict.

If you would rather work from your own two notes, the range test measures them in about a minute in the browser, then hands them straight to the full song list.

Where these numbers come from

They are read out of MIDI transcriptions from the Lakh MIDI Dataset, CC BY 4.0, by Colin Raffel. Human beings typed those melodies in by ear and about a fifth of the songs transcribed twice have versions that disagree by a whole octave. Only songs with two agreeing transcriptions and a range a voice can produce made it here, and some bad octaves still got through. The main page names one it still gets wrong.
