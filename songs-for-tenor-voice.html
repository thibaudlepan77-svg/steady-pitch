Songs for a tenor voice

170 songs whose melody stays inside C3 to C5 from start to finish, out of 432 measured. Allow three semitones of transposition either way and it becomes 245.

Most rock and pop was written for this range, so the constraint is rarely whether a song fits. It is whether the top of it sits above your break, which this list cannot see. A song topping out at G4 is comfortable for one tenor and a wrestling match for another, and only you know which.

Why a voice type is a bad way to pick songs

A voice type is not a range. It is where your voice sits happily and where it changes gear, and two people called tenor can have their break in different places. A choir director put it to me more bluntly than that, and he was right. Using C3 to C5 as a filter is a starting point, not a verdict.

If you would rather work from your own two notes, the range test measures them in about a minute in the browser, then hands them straight to the full song list.

Where these numbers come from

They are read out of MIDI transcriptions from the Lakh MIDI Dataset, CC BY 4.0, by Colin Raffel. Human beings typed those melodies in by ear and about a fifth of the songs transcribed twice have versions that disagree by a whole octave. Only songs with two agreeing transcriptions and a range a voice can produce made it here, and some bad octaves still got through. The main page names one it still gets wrong.
